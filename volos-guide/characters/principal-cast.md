# Principal Cast — Volo's Guide to Cape Velen

This file records the developed character canon relevant to Volo's journey. Do not silently add surnames, titles, relationships, or biographies that were not approved.

## Volothamp Geddarm

Volo is both traveler and narrative lens. His Cape Velen journey begins with skepticism.

Before arriving, he has researched Aldric Thorne and knows:

- Aldric was Admiral of the fleet.
- He overthrew Duke Calchais in a violent revolution in 1480 DR.
- His regime is deeply associated with Aurumandur and *The Thousand Truths*.
- Cape Velen is militant, expansionist/reclamation-minded, and aggressively recruiting settlers.
- Much of the region refuses to recognize the regime formally.

Volo therefore arrives wondering whether he is visiting a promising frontier or a zealot's duchy.

His journey is designed to change certainty into understanding rather than simply convert him into a propagandist.

## Duke Aldric Thorne

**Role:** Duke of Cape Velen; former Admiral of Cape Velen's fleet; lawful good paladin; deeply spiritual adherent of Aurumandur's teachings.

**Appearance:** A seasoned sailor-warrior rather than a pampered monarch. He came up through the ranks through blood and fire. His visual identity should communicate command, maritime experience, physical capability, and hard-earned authority. His official garb and livery are **ivory and gold**, adopted after the revolution in honor of Aurumandur, the Thousand Truths.

**History:** Aldric earned his reputation in the Nelanther campaigns against Port Skaug corsairs and other maritime threats. He preferred naval war in part because combat at sea more often confined violence to actual combatants. The civilian catastrophe of the Muranndin war appalled him: farmers and fishermen were exposed to orcs, hobgoblins, ogres, and other brutal occupiers.

He came to believe Duke Calchais's leadership was condemning Cape Velen. He had previously met the Noromathian leader Allain "Foxfire" Kevanarial and respected the elven commander's daring guerrilla campaign against Muranndin. Foxfire's resistance helped Aldric see that the duchy possessed a potential nation-within-its-borders whose alliance could change the strategic balance.

Aldric turned repeatedly to the Thousand Truths before deciding that inaction had become morally intolerable. In 1480 he led the revolution later called the Night of Blood.

**Leadership:** Aldric is a genuine leader of people. His Marines and sailors initially formed the core of the revolt; support expanded rapidly. As Duke he surrounds himself with capable people and trusts them rather than trying to rule every matter personally.

**Volo's evolution:** Volo knows of the coup before meeting Aldric. After experiencing Cape Velen and finally meeting the man, he begins to understand why this deeply spiritual leader concluded he had no other choice. This does not erase the bloodshed.

**Method with Volo:** Aldric teaches by arranging experiences rather than delivering lectures. He repeatedly puts Volo where he must see the stakes himself. The Duke's "cards" include forcing Volo to experience the dangerous Wealdath with Noromathian escorts before reaching Woodfaire.

## Duchess Talania Amaranthae Q'Thorne

**Role:** Duchess of Cape Velen; Aldric's Noromathian wife; mother of Markus; political and cultural bridge between the duchy and Noromathian people.

**Appearance developed from visual reference:** strikingly beautiful, red-haired, copper-skinned, unmistakably elven, dignified rather than ostentatious.

**Court presence:** Talania is not ornamental. She listens closely during petitions and quietly adds perspective. Her presence embodies the alliance as something deeper than a treaty. The marriage reads to Volo as grounded in mutual respect and shared purpose rather than merely political necessity.

**Leadership contrast:** Where Aldric naturally thinks in terms of immediate defense, fleets, roads, and survival, Talania represents longer elven horizons, cultural continuity, and consequences measured across generations.

## Lord Markus Thorne

**Role:** Son and heir of Aldric and Talania; half-elf; **Count of Firedrake County**.

Markus politely makes clear to Volo that the title does not mean he currently governs Firedrake County independently: his father rules it for now. Markus is learning and hopes to make his father proud.

**Appearance:** young half-elf; mother's elven features blended with father's build and bearing; dark curls and bright green eyes were developed in Volo's first-impression prose.

**Character:** attentive, thoughtful, curious, dutiful, and notably unentitled. During petitions he stands behind his father's throne, listens, takes notes, and learns. Aldric treats governance in Markus's presence as education.

**Symbolic role:** Markus is not merely the next duke. He is the living child of the human-Noromathian alliance and therefore represents a possible shared future.

## Lord Duncan the Damned

**Role:** Head/captain of the Duke's personal/Household Guard.

**Appearance:** enormous, powerfully built veteran; shaved/bald head; scarred; iron-gray facial hair in earlier prose; wears the ubiquitous white/ivory fur mantle fastened with the golden dragon brooch associated with Aldric's trusted inner circle.

**Presence:** suspicious, assessing, disciplined, protective. He is always working: scanning doors, counting exits, evaluating strangers. He rarely smiles not because he lacks humor but because vigilance is his profession.

**Epithet:** "the Damned" feels like an enemy-given title. Corsairs and other enemies should use it with fear. The image to evoke is that enemy sailors might whisper, "The Damned's aboard."

**Relationship to Aldric:** Duncan is one of the few people trusted enough to disagree openly with the Duke. His defining certainty is that nothing will happen to Aldric while Duncan still draws breath.

**Volo encounter:** Duncan first meets Volo in the palace main hall, eyes him suspiciously, then escorts him to the audience chamber. He remains quietly behind during the later terrace luncheon.

## Duke Allain "Foxfire" Kevanarial

**Full name:** **Allain Kevanarial**.

**People:** Noromathian elf.

**Title:** Humans, especially Tethyrians, insist upon styling him a Duke. Allain considers the title rather stupid but tolerates it for diplomacy. "Foxfire" is the name by which he is widely known.

**Appearance:** russet-haired Noromathian with **deep, endless brown eyes**. By 1492 DR he has aged and matured from earlier source depictions but remains essentially recognizable.

**History/role:** Foxfire has led guerrilla warfare against Muranndin for decades. Although Muranndin officially controls great stretches of northern Tethyr and the Wealdath, many occupation troops fear assignment to forest territory because of Foxfire and his rangers. His operations are daring, often deep behind enemy lines, and give little quarter to the occupying forces.

Aldric met him before the revolution and found in him a warrior he could respect. Foxfire's resistance helped inspire Aldric's realization that an alliance with the Noromathians could save Cape Velen.

## Countess Isolde Altaine

**Approved full name:** **Countess Isolde Altaine**.

The first name Isolde was selected deliberately for an older, established, frontier-noble cadence.

**Role in leadership ensemble:** competent senior noble/steward figure within Cape Velen. Earlier Volo work associated her with Monguldarath and the western/central administration. Preserve the exact developed location/title relationship from the POI ledger when integrating ledger data; do not invent additional holdings.

**Visual motif:** like Baron Conner and Duncan, she wears the white/ivory fur cloak/mantle with the golden dragon brooch, visually marking her as one of the Duke's trusted people and a public adherent to the Rebirth's ideals.

## Baron Aldus Conner

**Approved full name:** **Baron Aldus Conner**.

**Role:** frontier noble associated with Honorguard House/eastern defense in developed Volo material.

**Appearance:** visual reference emphasized the same ubiquitous white/ivory fur cloak with golden dragon brooch seen on Countess Altaine. This motif visually identifies members of Aldric's inner leadership circle.

**Function:** Conner represents the shield/frontier defense element of Aldric's leadership coalition.

## Gellan Primewater

**Source:** *Ghosts of Saltmarsh* character deliberately retained in the Cape Velen adaptation.

Gellan is the rich Saltmarsh merchant famous for lavish parties. Volo is invited to and attends one of Gellan's canonical-style parties. The Volo narrative includes his first-hand impressions of Gellan, the party, and the people he meets there. This belongs as an append-only expansion to the Saltmarsh travel entry; it is not a separate unrelated biography.

## Moonbrook

**Role:** one of the Noromathian escorts accompanying Volo through the western Wealdath.

Moonbrook dies during the ogre encounter on the morning of the third day, after Volo strays from the hidden camp. The death should be heroic and meaningful, ensuring that Volo's foolishness and the forest's danger have a real cost.

Do not resurrect or casually replace Moonbrook. The death is a key emotional event in Volo's understanding of the reclamation effort.

## Duke Calchais

**Role:** Duke of Cape Velen immediately before Aldric in the current approved Volo continuity.

Calchais's government is remembered as failing during the catastrophic Muranndin advance. Aldric believed his incompetence/inaction was costing civilian lives and ultimately chose revolution.

**Continuity warning:** earlier development elsewhere used other names/dates for a prior duke. For the recovered Volo workstream, the latest explicit approved continuity is **Duke Calchais, overthrown in 1480 DR**. Reconcile other GoV files only through an explicit canon decision; do not silently blend the versions.
