# Volo's Guide to Cape Velen

This directory is the authoritative home for the **Volo's Guide to Cape Velen** workstream within the **Ghosts of Velen (GoV)** project.

The purpose of this directory is continuity. It preserves the decisions, prose, locations, people, plots, themes, historical framework, source relationships, and unresolved development questions created during the original Volo conversation so that future work can resume without reconstructing prior context from memory.

## Authority and precedence

1. **Ghosts of Velen campaign canon** is authoritative for this adaptation.
2. **2024 Player's Handbook, 2024 Dungeon Master's Guide, and 2025 Monster Manual** are the preferred rules baseline ("5.5e").
3. Published Forgotten Realms material, especially **Lands of Intrigue**, supplies historical and regional foundations where it does not conflict with approved GoV canon.
4. **Ghosts of Saltmarsh** supplies adapted people, locations, situations, and maritime material where deliberately retained.
5. Material explicitly approved in the Volo development conversation overrides earlier drafts and unapproved suggestions.
6. Do not silently invent missing canon, reconcile contradictions, or import unused published NPCs. If a point is uncertain, mark it as unresolved.

## Current narrative spine

Volo is invited to Cape Velen by Duke Aldric Thorne. He arrives first in Velen but **does not meet the Duke**. After several weeks, he is unceremoniously placed aboard a frigate with a small guard and sent southwest around **the Bite** to **Saltmarsh**. His route then proceeds in order through **Tulmene**, **Monguldarath**, **Oakbottom**, **Jhaansciim**, **Honorguard House**, **Shoremeet**, **Tordraken**, the dangerous western marches of **the Wealdath**, and **Woodfaire**, before he finally travels north by road back to **Velen** and the ducal court.

The journey is deliberate. Aldric is not merely showing Volo picturesque destinations; he is making an argument through lived experience. Volo begins suspicious of a bloody revolutionary and near-messianic state organized around the teachings of an ancient gold dragon. He ends with a much more complicated view: Cape Velen is not a utopia, nor is Aldric infallible, but the duchy is attempting to build something difficult, dangerous, and worth understanding.

## Core themes

- **Hope is labor, not optimism.**
- **Strength exists to protect, not dominate.**
- **No community is utopia.** Good intentions still fail; grief and prejudice still wound people.
- **The western Wealdath is genuinely terrifying.** Reclamation is not a scenic return to an elven homeland; it is a dangerous struggle against a broken natural order, occupation scars, invasive monsters, and spiritual wounds.
- **Cape Velen has a strong Scottish-Highlander social character without costume parody.** Its people are plain-spoken, proud, practical, loyal, slow to trust, impatient with dandies, and deeply suspicious of empty boasting.
- **Aldric leads by showing rather than telling.** He rarely explains what Volo is meant to learn.
- **The guide is both travelogue and political inquiry.** Volo's skepticism matters.

## Immediate continuation point

The logistical journey is complete. Volo has returned to the capital, met the ducal family and court, eaten with them on the terrace above the harbor, slept at the palace, and departed by frigate the following morning after a quiet farewell from Duke Aldric.
